# Domain Context

## Glossary

### Redevelopment

The replacement or substantial reconstruction of an existing building occupied or
owned through a cooperative housing society in Maharashtra.

### Redevelopment Matter

A legal research matter concerning the redevelopment of a cooperative housing
society building in Maharashtra.

### Society-Led Self-Redevelopment

Redevelopment in which the cooperative housing society acts as the redevelopment
principal and retains control over the project instead of transferring that role
to a property developer.

### Cooperative Housing Society

A cooperative society registered in Maharashtra whose members hold occupancy
rights in residential or mixed-use premises managed by the society.

### Managing Committee

The elected body responsible for administering the cooperative housing society
and carrying out decisions validly made on its behalf.

### Primary User

The cooperative housing society or its managing committee seeking legal research
support for a society-led self-redevelopment matter.

### Research Assistance

Assistance that finds and explains legal authorities relevant to a
society-led self-redevelopment matter, with supporting citations.

### Compliance Assistance

Assistance that identifies applicable procedural requirements, produces
supporting checklists, and identifies missing evidence for a society-led
self-redevelopment matter.

### Applicable Date

The date on which the legal position relevant to a question or redevelopment
matter must be determined.

### Source Document

A document supplied to the assistant whose contents may be used as evidence in
research and compliance assistance.

## Scope

The initial knowledge corpus covers society-led self-redevelopment matters
involving cooperative housing societies in Maharashtra, with particular
practical relevance to Mumbai.

Developer-led redevelopment is outside the initial scope.

The initial assistant provides research assistance and compliance assistance.
Document review, drafting, and decision support are outside the initial scope.

The assistant answers from source documents supplied to it and does not
independently search for additional legal authorities.
